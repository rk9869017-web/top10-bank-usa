About Us

Your trusted source for understanding America's leading financial institutions. We provide data-driven insights, transparent rankings, and professional analysis.

Our Mission

At Top10Banks, our mission is to empower individuals, businesses, and investors with clear, accurate, and actionable information about the United States' most important financial institutions. We believe that understanding the banking landscape is essential for making informed financial decisions, whether you're choosing a checking account, evaluating investment opportunities, or analyzing systemic risk.

Our Data & Methodology

Our rankings are based on a comprehensive composite of publicly available data sourced from the following authoritative sources:

  • Federal Reserve Reports — Quarterly financial data, stress test results, and systemic risk indicators.
  • S&P Global Market Intelligence — Industry benchmarking, credit ratings, and market capitalization.
  • Independent Analyst Ratings — Customer satisfaction surveys, brand value assessments, and regulatory compliance scores.
  • FDIC & OCC Filings — Deposit and asset data, capital adequacy ratios, and insurance status.

We update our rankings quarterly to reflect the latest financial disclosures, regulatory changes, and market developments. Our composite scoring methodology assigns weight to assets (40%), market capitalization (20%), customer satisfaction (15%), regulatory standing (15%), and digital innovation (10%).
